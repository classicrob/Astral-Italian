ASTRAL ITALIAN - read me first
==============================

Astral Italian is a fan-made native Mac port of Super Mario Galaxy 2. It contains NO game data. You need your
own copy of the game: a disc image of the USA version (game ID SB4E01) as .iso, .wbfs or .rvz, or an
already extracted folder. Nobody here can supply it.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS __MINMACOS__ or newer, about 6 GB of free disk space.

1. Drag "Astral Italian.app" to your Applications folder.

2. First launch. This app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks it the first time:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer: double-click the app, click Done on the warning, then open
     System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Astral Italian was blocked", click "Open Anyway"
     and confirm with your password.
   - macOS 14 and older: right-click (Control-click) the app, choose Open, then click Open.
   - Or, in Terminal (works everywhere):
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Astral Italian.app"
   You only do this once.

3. The app then asks for your game disc. Pick the disc image (or the folder). It checks that it is the
   right game and copies what it needs into ~/Library/Application Support/Astral Italian/ (a few minutes).

4. Play. Press the key above Tab (`) or F1 for the menu: controls, mods and display settings.
   Something wrong? Press M (or F9) right after it happens, then run "Collect Bug Report.command"
   and attach the zip it makes to an issue: https://github.com/classicrob/Astral-Italian/issues/new/choose

Full guide: https://github.com/classicrob/Astral-Italian/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md
